# PJA V6.1 — Accepted Tryout Roster + AutoMod

## Accepted tryout roster
- Accepting a tryout application places the player in a staff-only Accepted Tryouts roster.
- Staff can promote the player to the main roster or remove them from Accepted Tryouts.
- Removing from Accepted Tryouts does not delete the archived application.
- Promotion removes the player from Accepted Tryouts and adds them to the main roster.

## AutoMod
- Manager Portal → AutoMod controls whether Discord invite links are allowed per server.
- Default is ON (invite links allowed).
- When OFF, non-staff invite links are deleted and the sender is warned.
- Staff are exempt.
- No bans, kicks, strikes, or point penalties are applied.
- The bot needs Manage Messages permission for deletion, and Message Content intent must remain enabled.

No new Discord slash commands were added.
